Privacy Policy

Murdrid: Solve the Slay · Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game Murdrid: Solve the Slay ("the game", "we") uses, why, and what choices you have. The game is made by Kevin Bellens, trading as RiffBolt, Belgium ("we", "us"). Contact: riffbolt1@gmail.com.

Short version

  • You do not need to give us your name, e-mail or any other personal details to play.
  • Your progress is saved on your device and, so you never lose it, online with Unity Gaming Services under an anonymous player account.
  • Free players see ads. Our ad partner may use your device's advertising identifier, only as far as you allow it.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
  • You can delete your online progress at any time in the game: Settings › Save & transfer › Delete online progress.

1. Information we use

Game progress (online save)

We save your progress (solved cases, stars, scores, opened folders, Premium status and its store receipt) on your device. To protect it and to let you move it to another device, it is also stored with Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) under an anonymous player ID that Unity creates. If you create a transfer code, a generated username and password are linked to that anonymous account; they contain no personal information.

Purpose: providing the game and keeping your progress. Legal basis (GDPR): performance of our agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b)).

Advertising

Free players see video ads, served through Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with. To show ads, these partners may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device type, operating system and how you interact with ads. On iOS we only use the IDFA if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before ads are personalised; without consent you see non-personalised ads. Premium players see no ads.

Purpose: showing ads that pay for the free game. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for personalised ads; our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) for non-personalised ads and fraud prevention.

Purchases

Premium is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment; we receive only a receipt that confirms the purchase, which we use to unlock and restore Premium.

Time check

To keep energy timers fair, the game reads the current time from a public internet server (a plain request to google.com that sends no game or personal data, apart from the IP address every internet request carries).

Things we do not do

  • We do not ask for or store your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools beyond what is described above.

2. Service providers

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services, Unity LevelPlay) – privacy policy
  • Ad networks working with Unity LevelPlay – listed at Unity's player privacy page
  • Apple App Store / Google Play – for purchases, under their own privacy policies

Some of these providers process data outside the EEA (for example in the United States). They do so under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

3. How long we keep it

Online progress is kept as long as you play. If you delete your online progress in the game, it is removed from Unity's servers. Online progress that is not used for 24 months may be deleted. Progress on your device is removed when you uninstall the game.

4. Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, object to its use, or withdraw consent at any time (for ads: in the game's settings or your device's privacy settings). Most of this you can do yourself in the game. Otherwise, write to riffbolt1@gmail.com and include your player ID (shown in Settings › Save & transfer). You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes are announced in the game.
